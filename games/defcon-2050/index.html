Defcon 2050

Place your defenses. Win the ocean. Time the launch.
A closer take on classic DEFCON strategy, with real geography and step-by-step command guidance.

01 / Deployment & detectionPlace silos, radar and airbases in North America; navy in the western Atlantic. Keep overlapping silo defenses around cities.
02 / Conventional warAt DEFCON 3, battleships engage ships and aircraft. Carriers launch aircraft or hunt submarines. Send fighters to reveal enemy assets.
03 / Nuclear releaseAt DEFCON 1, change silos to ICBM mode. Switching takes 20 seconds and disables defense. Queue targets; submarines must surface to fire.
04 / Coordinate your attackBombers fly into launch range and return to base. Combine bombers, submarines and silo salvos to overwhelm enemy defenses.

Each DEFCON phase lasts 90 simulated seconds after deployment. Game speed starts at 1×; choose 5× or 15× to accelerate. Matches end with a 3-minute victory countdown at low arsenal levels, or at 24:00. Space pauses for orders. Sounds start after you press Assume command.

Command reference / game paused

FIELD MANUAL

Silos & radarSilos intercept visible incoming nukes and aircraft in Air Defense mode. ICBM mode has a 20s switch and a 12s launch interval. Radar extends detection; it cannot shoot.
Airbases & carriersSelect Fighter patrol, then click an area to scout and engage. Select Bomber strike, then an enemy target. Planes have fuel and return automatically. Carriers cannot launch aircraft while using sonar.
Battleships & submarinesBattleships attack aircraft and surface ships; submerged submarines torpedo battleships and carriers. Sonar carriers attack submarines. These attacks are automatic within range from DEFCON 3. Select a ship then click water, or drag the ship to move. Submarines are hidden unless close to a sonar carrier, firing, or surfaced. Surface mode takes 15s.
Orders & targetingLeft-click your unit, use its action button, then click the map. For ships, click open water after selection, or drag the ship. Right-click also issues orders. Shift-click queues multiple targets. Clear orders cancels queued attacks. Zoom in for crowded units.

Blue circles show detection; gold dashed circles show weapon range. Gold halos mark detected units your selected unit can attack automatically once ready. The unit panel lists targets, limitations and readiness. A dashed enemy building is an old contact, so its current status is unknown. City labels appear when you hover or zoom. Launches reveal their origin.
