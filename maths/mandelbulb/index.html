About the Mandelbulb

What is it?

Constructed in 2009 by Daniel White and Paul Nylander, the Mandelbulb maps the classic 2D equation vn+1 = vnN + c into 3D space by decomposing points into spherical coordinates (radius r, polar angle θ, and azimuthal angle φ).

Why is it special?

  • Organic Geometry: Rather than rigid cubic architecture, it blossoms into alien coral, biological filaments, and botanical broccoli-like lobes.
  • Power Evolution: At power 2 it resembles simple bulbous eggs, but at power 8 it explodes into hyper-dense filigree with endless recursive detail.
