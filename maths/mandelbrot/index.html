About the Fractal

What is it?

Points c on the complex plane calculated with the repeating rule zn+1 = zn2 + c (starting at 0). Numbers that remain trapped forever form the black interior; escaping numbers are coloured by how quickly they break free.

Why is it special?

  • Infinite Depth: A simple, elementary equation generates an endless, non-repeating boundary.
  • Recursive Copies: Zooming in reveals miniature Mandelbrot sets ("minibrots"), each subtly sculpted by its local surroundings.
  • Edge of Chaos: It marks the universal frontier where predictable mathematical order dissolves into deterministic chaos.
A Sense of Scale: Zooming in by 1014× is like shrinking a human being down to the size of an atomic nucleus. This explorer calculates with full double precision, letting you reach that exact subatomic scale seamlessly.
