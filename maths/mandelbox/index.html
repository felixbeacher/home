About the Mandelbox

What is it?

Discovered by Tom Lowe in 2010, the Mandelbox is a true 3D fractal. Each 3D point is recursively reflected through a cube (box fold) and inverted through a sphere (sphere fold), then scaled and translated.

Why is it special?

  • Alien Architecture: Geometry forms vast colonnades, organic cathedrals, structural pillars, and mechanical grids.
  • Infinite 3D Depth: Zooming in reveals that every pillar and beam is constructed from smaller pillars and beams, echoing endlessly inwards.
