Essential Things to Keep in Mind

Usually, dressings must remain strictly dry for the first 48 hours post-operation. After this period, follow your surgeon's specific discharge instructions regarding waterproof dressings or showering rules. Never submerge the leg in a bath or swimming pool until the wound is fully closed and stitches/staples are removed.
Gravity causes fluid to accumulate in the lower limb. Elevating your leg above the level of your heart encourages venous return, reduces swelling (oedema), relieves throbbing pain, and reduces tension on your surgical stitches, which prevents wound gapping.
Non-dissolvable sutures or metal staples on the leg are usually removed 10 to 14 days after surgery by a practice nurse or district nurse. Dissolvable stitches usually fade within 2 to 6 weeks.
