Physics interpretation
Live readouts use the Schwarzschild gravitational redshift for stationary clocks, √(1 − rₛ/r). The craft has its own freefall proper-time clock; the two are not interchangeable. Black-hole horizon crossing happens in finite craft proper time, although distant Schwarzschild coordinates behave differently. Orbital motion, lensing and disc light are deliberately illustrative rather than full general-relativistic ray tracing. There is no event horizon around a neutron star.
